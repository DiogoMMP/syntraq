# Question catalog of {{COURSE}}

Every question of the past exams of {{COURSE}}, one row per question or sub-question. The analysis is in [[Exams of {{COURSE}}]].

- **Id** — sitting letter (N normal, R recurso, E especial, M model, T test) + start year of the school year + question, e.g. `N23-P3b`. A second paper of the same kind in the same year adds a letter after the year (`T23b-P1`; `Mb-P1` for a second model exam). Ids never change once given.
- **Summary** — one line on what the question asks, not its full text; the paper is linked.
- **Kind** — explain/compare/comment, calculation, trace an execution, write code or rules, draw, multiple choice, …
